# Absence, vacuum, and particle-like excitation

MOSAIC does not model absolute nothingness as an object or particle. A claim of
absence is meaningful only relative to a declared target and scope: nothing
of what, where, when, and under which rules?

## Three different representations

1. **Absence claim (AbsenceClaim)** — a scoped epistemic or logical statement.
   - **NOT_DETECTED** means a declared search did not detect the target. The
     method, coverage, and optional detection limit bound what can be inferred.
     It does not establish universal nonexistence.
   - **EMPTY_BY_DEFINITION** is permitted only when the domain is explicitly
     declared closed. This is a statement about that defined domain.
   - **FORBIDDEN_BY_MODEL** identifies the model whose rules exclude the target.
     This does not establish that the model describes physical reality.
   - **UNKNOWN** makes no absence claim.
2. **Vacuum state (PhysicalStateReference)** — a state called vacuum inside a
   named physical model. It is not an assertion that spacetime, fields, laws,
   or all possible structure are absent.
3. **Particle-like excitation (ParticleExcitation)** — a proposed excitation
   relative to a named model and reference state. The representation is not
   itself detector evidence, a proof, or a claim that a particle can emerge
   from philosophical nothingness.

## The phrase “particle of nothing”

The phrase is not a well-defined physical entity on its own. In quantum field
theory, particle language is commonly tied to excitations of fields relative to
a chosen state; the vacuum is the reference state, not literal nonbeing. The
precise particle concept can depend on the theory, background, and observer.
MOSAIC therefore requires a model identifier and reference-state identifier
before recording a particle-like excitation.

An absence of detections is also not the same as a vacuum state. A detector can
fail to register something because of limited sensitivity, incomplete coverage,
or an unsuitable measurement. Conversely, a model can define a vacuum even
though it contains fields and nontrivial structure.

## Reasoning boundary

These schemas organize claims and their provenance. They do not calculate
quantum-field dynamics, establish a particle discovery, or settle the
metaphysics of nothingness. A future physics backend would need explicit
mathematical structures, units, state definitions, measurement operators,
and independently checkable predictions. A hash can protect evidence identity;
it cannot make the evidence true.
